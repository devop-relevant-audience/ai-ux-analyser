THE PROCESS

Accessibility and performance are evaluated using objective evidence.

04

SEVEN UX DIMENSIONS

Explore what we look at.

Hover over a dimension to discover what it means.

Visual Hierarchy

How the interface guides user's attention effectively using clear hierarchy of contents.

Navigation

How easily users can find their way around the webpage.

Aesthetic Design

How the interface presents a visually coherent and balanced design without interfering with content clarity.

Consistency & Standards

How consistently the webpage uses visual patterns and follows an established design standards.

Clarity & Familiarity

How clearly the webpage communicates information using understandable language and familiar patterns.

Accessibility

How effectively the interface can be perceived and understood by users with diverse abilities.

Performance

How effectively the webpage performs and responds during use.

THE EVIDENCE

What the AI uses to evaluate.

The evaluation is grounded in evidence collected directly from the webpage, combining visual observations with objective evidence.

01

Visual Evidence

Screenshots are captured across mobile, tablet, and desktop, providing visual evidence of the webpage's presentation and user experience.

02

Accessibility Audit

Axe identifies technical accessibility issues and provides objective findings from the webpage.

03

Performance Audit

Lighthouse provides objective performance metrics from the webpage across mobile and desktop.
